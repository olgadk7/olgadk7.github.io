Hi! I'm a self-taught data scientist and solo founder. I build micro-SaaS tools that take a big, boring wall of data — the kind no human wants to read — and turn it into a clear map you can act on. My road here wasn't straight: modeling, politics and economics, art PR, several countries, before I found data science and, lately, AI. What carried me through every reinvention is the same thing that drives every product I build: a need to get to the truth, strip out bias, and not be fooled — so I can spend that clarity on living fully. If you ask for evidence, question the programs you were handed, and believe life is too short to live on autopilot, you're my kind of person. *Never lose the thread.*

## What I'm building — Ozzie Dynamics

A one-person studio shipping focused tools that each turn messy data into a clear, actionable map.

**Live now**

**[Helse](https://gethelse.com)** — *health.* Your health data is scattered — lab PDFs, clinic portals, wearables, supplement bottles — and nobody's holding the thread. Helse pulls it all into one proactive picture, graded against *optimal* ranges rather than just "normal," so you can get ahead of problems instead of reacting to them. Anything worth watching becomes a tracked concern, with every result, appointment, and note on one timeline — and you can keep a separate profile for each person (or pet) you look after, not only yourself. *Stop reacting to your health. Start staying ahead of it.*

**[Bobbin](https://heybobbin.com)** — *knowledge.* Paste in any text — notes, an article, a long AI chat — and it weaves the ideas into a navigable concept graph you can read as a digest, a table, or an interactive map.

**In the works**

**Pantry** — *food.* A recipe engine that filters by what's already in your kitchen and what you're in the mood for.

**Thoughtstream** — *capture.* Dictate a thought on the go; it sorts it into tasks, ideas, and a nightly brief, and routes it where it belongs.

**Bare** — *skincare.* Decodes the products you own at the ingredient level, not the brand — what your skin actually needs, and whether that next purchase is worth it.
