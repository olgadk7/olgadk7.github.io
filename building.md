# Ozzie Dynamics

I'm a solo founder building a small portfolio of focused tools that all do one thing: take a pile of messy, unreadable data and turn it into a clear map you can act on. Different corners of life — health, knowledge, food — same idea. *Never lose the thread.*

## Live now

**[Bobbin](https://heybobbin.com)** — *knowledge.* Paste in any text — notes, an article, a long AI chat — and it weaves the ideas into a navigable concept graph you can read as a digest, a table, or an interactive map.

**[Helse](https://gethelse.com)** — *health.* Pulls your scattered health data — lab PDFs, clinic reports, wearables, supplements, meds — into one proactive picture, graded against *optimal* ranges, not just "normal," with a timeline for everyone you look after.

## In the works

**Pantry** — *food.* A recipe engine that filters by what's already in your kitchen and what you're in the mood for.

**Thoughtstream** — *capture.* Dictate a thought on the go; it sorts it into tasks, ideas, and a nightly brief, and routes it where it belongs.

**Bare** — *skincare.* Decodes the products you own at the ingredient level, not the brand — what your skin actually needs, and whether that next purchase is worth it.
